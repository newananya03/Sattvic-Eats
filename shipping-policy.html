Shipping Policy

How we get your order to you.

Last Updated: 16 August 2026

At Sattvic Eats, we carefully pack our products so that they reach you safely and in good condition. This Shipping Policy applies to orders placed through the Sattvic Eats website.

1. Shipping Locations

Sattvic Eats currently delivers to serviceable locations across India. Delivery availability depends on the serviceability of the customer's PIN code.

2. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after successful payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays, public holidays or during periods of exceptionally high order volume may require additional processing time.

3. Delivery Time

The estimated delivery time is generally 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery times may vary depending on:

  • Delivery location
  • Courier availability
  • Weather conditions
  • Public holidays
  • Transport disruptions
  • Incorrect or incomplete address information
  • Other circumstances beyond our reasonable control

The delivery timeline is an estimate and not a guaranteed delivery date.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges will be displayed at checkout before the order is completed. Sattvic Eats may offer free shipping or other shipping promotions from time to time. Any such offer will be subject to the terms displayed during the promotion.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking information may be provided through email, SMS or WhatsApp. You can use the tracking information to follow your shipment.

6. Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate and complete delivery address and contact number. If an order is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer, additional shipping charges may apply for re-delivery, where available.

7. Delayed Delivery

If your order is delayed, please contact us at contact.sattviceats@gmail.com or 9535476030 (Phone/WhatsApp). We will coordinate with the delivery partner and provide an update.

8. Damaged or Tampered Package

If your package appears visibly damaged, opened or tampered with at delivery:

  • Take photographs or video of the package.
  • If possible, refuse delivery of a visibly compromised package.
  • Contact Sattvic Eats as soon as possible.

If you accept the package, retain the packaging and product until the matter has been reviewed.

9. Food Products

Sattvic Eats sells food products including:

  • Arabica filter coffee powder
  • Badam powder
  • Dry fruits
  • Seeds
  • Dry fruits and seeds laddus
  • Other food products listed on our website

We take reasonable care in packaging and dispatching food products. Applicable food information and declarations will be provided on the product packaging and/or product page as required.
