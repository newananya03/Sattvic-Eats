Return & Refund Policy

Simple, transparent and fair to you.

Last Updated: 16 August 2026

At Sattvic Eats, we take care to pack and dispatch our products in good condition. Since our products are food items, including dry fruits, seeds, coffee, powders and laddus, we follow a specific return and refund policy to protect product quality and food safety.

1. When Can You Request a Return or Refund?

We may accept a return, replacement or refund request if you receive:

  • A wrong product
  • A missing product
  • A damaged product
  • A product with damaged or compromised packaging
  • A product that appears spoiled or unsuitable for consumption
  • A product that has passed its best-before/use-by date at the time of delivery
  • A product materially different from the product ordered

Each request will be reviewed by our customer-care team.

2. Time Limit for Reporting an Issue

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if you receive a damaged, incorrect or defective product. For any concern relating to food quality, spoilage or safety, please contact us as soon as you notice the issue. If you believe a product may be unsafe, please do not consume it.

3. Information Required

To help us process your request quickly, please provide:

  • Order number
  • Name and registered mobile number
  • Photographs of the outer package
  • Photographs of the product
  • Photograph of the batch/lot number
  • Photograph showing the best-before/expiry information
  • Description of the problem
  • Unboxing video, where available

We may request additional information if required to investigate the complaint.

4. Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged:

  • Take clear photographs of the package and product.
  • Keep the original packaging.
  • Contact us within 48 hours.
  • Do not consume the product if you believe it has been compromised.

After reviewing the claim, we may offer a replacement or refund, depending on the circumstances.

5. Wrong or Missing Product

If you receive a product different from what you ordered, or an item is missing from your order, contact us within 48 hours. After verification, we may:

  • Send the correct product;
  • Send the missing product; or
  • Provide an appropriate refund for the affected item.

6. Food Quality Concerns

If you believe a product is spoiled, contaminated or otherwise unsuitable for consumption:

  • Stop using the product immediately.
  • Do not consume it.
  • Keep the product and packaging safely stored.
  • Contact us with the order and batch details.
  • Provide photographs and other relevant information.

We may investigate the matter and, where appropriate, coordinate with our suppliers, logistics partners or other relevant parties.

7. Products That Cannot Normally Be Returned

Because Sattvic Eats sells food products, we generally do not accept returns for:

  • Change of mind
  • Taste preference
  • Personal dislike of a product
  • Incorrect product selection by the customer
  • Improper storage after delivery
  • Damage caused by the customer after delivery
  • Products that have been opened or consumed, except where the complaint relates to a genuine quality or safety issue
  • Claims made substantially after the applicable reporting period without a reasonable explanation

This does not affect any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

8. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount will normally be processed to the original payment method, where technically possible. The time required for the refund to appear in your bank account, card or payment account may depend on the payment gateway and financial institution.

9. Replacement

If a replacement is approved, Sattvic Eats may dispatch the replacement product at no additional product cost. Replacement is subject to product availability. If the same product is unavailable, we may provide an alternative resolution, including a refund where appropriate.

10. Order Cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible after placing it. If the order has not yet been processed or dispatched, we will make reasonable efforts to cancel it. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

11. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

Where an eligible cancellation is approved, the applicable refund will normally be processed through the original payment method. Any refund will be subject to applicable payment-gateway processing requirements.

12. Return Shipping

Where the issue is attributable to Sattvic Eats, such as receiving a wrong or damaged product, we will determine the appropriate resolution and may arrange the required return or replacement process. Where an issue is not eligible under this policy, the customer may be responsible for any return or additional shipping costs.

13. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict or remove any consumer rights available under applicable Indian laws. The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 contain provisions applicable to e-commerce businesses concerning goods that do not match advertised or agreed specifications and applicable consumer remedies.
